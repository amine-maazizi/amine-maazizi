# Amine Maazizi

I'm a postgraduate researcher in machine learning & computer vision (MVA) from ENS Paris-Saclay and a research engineer graduate from ENSTA Paris. Currently conducting research on foundation model-based perturbation modeling on spatial proteomics data at EPFL's Artificial Intelligence for Molecular Medicine Lab, I'll be joining Mines Paris Centre of Robotics starting November 2026 for my PhD in robotics and 3D vision co-supervised by the U2SI lab of ENSTA Paris.

## Research Interests

My research interests are representation learning, 3D vision, and multimodal knowledge transfer.
