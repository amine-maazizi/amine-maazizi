# Amine Maazizi

I'm a PhD student in 3D vision and robotics at the Centre de Robotique, Mines Paris – PSL, co-supervised by the U2SI lab of ENSTA Paris. I'm working on 3D perception and representation learning for autonomous robotics in natural and unstructured environments. 

## Research Interests

My research interests are cross-modal and self-supervised learning for robust 3D perception.
